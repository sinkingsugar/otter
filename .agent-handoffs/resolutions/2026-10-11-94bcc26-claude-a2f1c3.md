## 2026-10-11-357fba2-codex-167fcb#F1
- Disposition: fixed
- Change/reason: `Mesh::tick` drains requests before stepping (requests made between ticks end their targets before anything runs) and after (requests made during the tick); `finish` lets a pending request decide a `Completed` or `Stopped` outcome (`Failed` stays). A target activated in the tick a request was made in still has that activation: the rule is documented in `wire_instance.rs`, `docs/host-requirements.md` H5 and `docs/current-state.md`. Checking requests before every activation would cost about two instructions per activation; that alternative is put to the maintainer.
- Evidence: `prototype::a_request_wins_over_a_completion_in_the_same_tick` covers the review's three reproductions under both wake modes (host request before the first tick: Stopped, never instantiated; `Stop(w)` before the child's first activation: Wait fails `wire-stopped`; owner ending before its child's first activation: child Cancelled). Fails on 357fba2, passes at the fix.
