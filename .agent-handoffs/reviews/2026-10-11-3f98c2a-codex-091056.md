## F1 — P1 `arith_into` and `compare_into` alias the accumulator with the numeric slot they write
- Location: `crates/otter-core/src/inline.rs`, `arith_into`/`compare_into` and their call sites in `run` (at 3f98c2a).
- Evidence: after any numeric-producing instruction the accumulator is the numeric slot. The helpers received it as `value: &Var` and wrote that storage through `numeric`; reading first does not end a shared parameter's protection during the call. Examples: `1 | Math.Add(1) | Math.Multiply(2)`, `1 | Math.Add(1) | IsMore(0)`. Same class as [2026-10-11-dcee86c-claude-0b393d#F1](2026-10-11-dcee86c-claude-0b393d.md).
- Impact: undefined behavior on ordinary arithmetic chains, runtime and metered.
- Suggested direction: pass the accumulator as a raw pointer, form short-lived references inside, finish computing, then write; Miri tests for both chains under both borrow models.

## F2 — P2 A string-key Take skips the output check
- Location: the hot `Take` arm's `continue 'run` after `take_key_into`.
- Evidence: the success path continued the loop past the common `check_output`.
- Impact: in debug and `output-checks` builds a key read's declared output type is never validated.
- Suggested direction: route success through the common check; test a deliberately mismatched declared output.

## Other conclusions (no finding)
